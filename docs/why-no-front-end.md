# Why there is no front end

This base serves the reporting operator. They bring recorded exports across, review evidence gaps, assign follow-ups and produce working papers. A database and commands cover that desk without a new application to maintain.

B2Gnow also provides vendor portals, certification workflows, directories, reporting and other modules. Those are real capabilities. This focused base does not reproduce the whole platform. Keep the services your programme needs until replacements have been separately specified, built and checked.

The read-only HTML reports are for review and printing. They provide no mobile capture, offline forms, drag and drop, vendor self-service or authentication. Shared operations need enforced access controls, backups and a retention process. Enterprise DNA can scope the screens and connections in your version.

Use /new-view to add a report and /customise to change the records through a tested migration. Keep government submissions, certification decisions, payroll and payment execution in their authorised systems.

Built and optionally operated through Omni by Enterprise DNA.
