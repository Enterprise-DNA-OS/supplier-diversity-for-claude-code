The [City of Savannah November 2025 record](https://agenda.savannahga.gov/agendaitem/previewagendaitem?agendaitemid=11802) documents US$49,157 for annual renewals and US$54,257 for the initial purchase including setup, configuration and training. This is a US public-sector procurement example, not a current vendor list price and not evidence of a 10 to 50 person NZ/AU customer bill. Local customer and CRM repository searches established no B2Gnow usage or invoice. No live CRM access was used. Buyer fit is explicitly low and remains unverified.

[B2Gnow vendor management](https://b2gnow.com/solutions/vendor-management-software/) describes certificate tracking, vendor utilisation and reporting exports. The current public pages reviewed offer a sales conversation rather than a current general list price. An old 2019 price sheet was rejected as current pricing. The comparison page therefore uses no dollar figures and has an empty prices list.

[SkillSmart's B2Gnow comparison](https://www.skillsmart.us/b2g-now-alternative/) demonstrates alternative-search positioning by a real rival. It is evidence of competition for the phrase, not measured search demand. No rival performance claims are copied.

[ReliaSoft Weibull++](https://cloud.hbkworld.com/en/products/software/reliability/weibull-life-data-analysis-software) describes lifetime distributions, warranty and degradation analysis. [Precoro pricing](https://precoro.com/pricing) lists its Automation starting point at US$999 a month billed annually. Neither was selected.
